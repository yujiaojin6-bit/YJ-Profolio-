Caribou Coffee

Loyalty Growth Strategy

How can Casual Loyalists visit two more times each year?

ApproachDescriptive analysis, PSM and DID across a 2M+ member loyalty database, supported by survey and secondary research.

Key insightApp and order-ahead behavior revealed specific moments where habitual visits could become more frequent.

Strategic implicationThree strategic themes became six targeted campaign action plans for loyalty and digital growth.

02 / 04

Alo Yoga

Brand Strategy & Consumer Research

Where does the brand promise meet the consumer reality?

ApproachPrimary consumer research, audience segmentation and competitive analysis mapped intended positioning against lived perception.

Key insightThe brand held strong cultural equity, but meaningful gaps remained between aspiration, differentiation and audience fit.

Strategic implicationSharper positioning and influencer strategy could turn brand heat into a clearer long-term growth system.

View Case Study
03 / 04

TikTok

Machine Learning & Reach Prediction

Which creator and content signals predict reach?

ApproachOLS, Ridge/Lasso and Random Forest models tested content and creator-level drivers across 12K+ videos.

Key insightReach was not explained by a single engagement metric; a combination of creator context and content signals mattered.

Strategic implicationPrediction can help teams prioritize creative inputs before publishing, not only report performance after the fact.

View Case Study
04 / 04

Yelp

Restaurant Performance Analytics

What makes a restaurant visible, credible and built to last?

ApproachLinear and logistic regression, odds-ratio interpretation, likelihood-ratio tests and model selection.

Key insightUser credibility, pricing, location and review volume work together as practical signals of ratings and survival.

Strategic implicationRestaurant operators can focus on the levers that shape both discoverability and durable customer trust.
